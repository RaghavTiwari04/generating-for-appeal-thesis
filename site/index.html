Research prototype. Not a card shop, and nothing here is for sale.

Can a model learn what makes a birthday card worth choosing?

This is the working half of an MSc thesis. It reads a few thousand cards that already sell, writes its own briefs, draws the artwork with the greeting lettered into it, and then tries to guess which card people would want.

The guessing is the part I could not check. So the demo makes four cards and asks you to pick one.
